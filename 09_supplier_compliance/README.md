# Supplier compliance series

**Bilingual checklists for Taiwanese semiconductor and AI-hardware suppliers setting up in the United States.**
給赴美設點的台灣半導體與 AI 硬體供應商：中英對照的法遵清單。

A company that follows its customer to a U.S. fab site meets a familiar set of legal questions early, often before it has a lawyer on staff. Each issue of this series takes one of those questions and adds one checklist or template that a company can adapt, in English and Traditional Chinese.

## Issues

| Issue | Released | Question | Tool | Article |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Planned for January 15, 2027 | Selling into a U.S. fab: which of the customer's terms pass down to a supplier? | Flow-down checklist, English and Chinese | Linked here after publication |

Further issues follow every two months from April 2027, on the 15th.

## What an issue contains

- The checklist or template, in English and Traditional Chinese, as Markdown and PDF.
- A short note: what the tool is for, how to adapt it, and what it leaves out.
- The public sources it relies on, with dates.

Each issue has its own folder (for example `2027-01_flow-down/`) and its own release of this repository, so a specific version can be cited.

Related articles are written for outside publications and are not stored here. When one is published, the table links to it.

## Scope

- The tools are built from public statutes, regulations and agency guidance, and from fictional examples. Nothing comes from an employer's or a client's documents.
- They are general information, not legal advice. A checklist helps a company prepare its questions for a lawyer admitted in the relevant state; it does not replace that lawyer.
- Views are my own and do not represent my employer.
