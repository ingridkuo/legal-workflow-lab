# Supplier compliance series

**Bilingual checklists for Taiwanese semiconductor and AI-hardware suppliers setting up in the United States.**
給赴美設點的台灣半導體與 AI 硬體供應商：中英對照的法遵清單。

A company that follows its customer to a U.S. fab site meets a familiar set of legal questions early, often before it has a lawyer on staff. Each issue of this series takes one of those questions and adds one checklist or template that a company can adapt, in English and Traditional Chinese. Where state law matters, the series starts with Arizona.

## Already in the Lab

Until the first issue, these tools elsewhere in this repository are the ones a supplier can use now. They were written before the series, mostly in English, with fictional parties and figures; where they cite state law, check the state you are in.

- [Supply terms in two languages](../02_legal_ops/Commercial_Contracting_Handbook.md#bilingual-supply-terms): the English position, the Chinese point beside it, and the decision each row records.
- [Hardware supply issues, from the buyer's side](../02_legal_ops/Commercial_Contracting_Handbook.md#hardware-supplier-issues-buyer-version): what a buyer asks for, the evidence it expects and when it escalates, so a supplier can prepare its answers.
- [MSA and SOW checklist](../02_legal_ops/Commercial_Contracting_Handbook.md#msa-and-sow-checklist): which document governs, and what to settle before work starts.
- [Construction subcontract checklist](../02_legal_ops/Commercial_Contracting_Handbook.md#construction-subcontract-checklist), with the [subcontract workflow and its Excel tracker](../07_construction_workflow/README.md): approvals, signature order and the dates to watch after signing.
- [Contract operations tracker (Excel)](../02_legal_ops/Contract_Operations_Tracker.xlsx): obligations, renewals and payments after signature.

## What an issue contains

- The checklist or template, in English and Traditional Chinese, as Markdown and PDF.
- A short note: what the tool is for, how to adapt it, and what it leaves out.
- The public sources it relies on, with the date each was read.

Each issue has its own folder (for example `2027-01_flow-down/`) and its own release of this repository, so a specific version can be cited.

Related articles are written for outside publications and are not stored here. When one is published, the table links to it.

## Scope

- The tools are built from public statutes, regulations, agency guidance and contract forms that companies publish, and from fictional examples. Nothing comes from an employer's or a client's documents.
- They are general information, not legal advice. A checklist helps a company prepare its questions for a lawyer admitted in the relevant state; it does not replace that lawyer.
- Views are my own and do not represent my employer.
