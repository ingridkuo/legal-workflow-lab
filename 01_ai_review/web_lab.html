How to read these results

TP requires a matching document/rule pair and an exact quotation in its cited Section. FP counts unmatched or unsupported returned findings; FN counts missed reference issues. Precision = TP/(TP+FP); recall = TP/(TP+FN). A zero denominator is undefined.

Agreement measures repeatability, not correctness. A stable miss remains a miss.

These three independent agent batches reuse one synthetic set. Model snapshot and sampling settings were not exposed. This is not a controlled API study or a production integration.

Every review retains a human-review gate. Legal interpretation and authorization remain with the reviewer.
