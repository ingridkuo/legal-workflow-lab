## NDA review playbook

| Issue | Standard | Fallback | Escalate and owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Term and trade secrets | Five years for ordinary information after expiry or termination; trade secrets protected while qualifying under applicable law. | Three years after expiry or termination for ordinary information, with Legal approval; preserve the trade-secret distinction. | No survival, rolling short expiry, or a finite trade-secret cutoff. Legal + IP owner. Five years is a policy assumption, not a statutory term. |
| Purpose and AI use | Use only to evaluate the stated project. No shared-model training or unrelated commercial use. | Clarify purpose and permitted systems. Broader use needs Legal review; no training permission implied. | Broader internal-business or commercialization use: YELLOW Legal review. Shared-model training or personal data: RED specialist hold; any RED trigger prevails. |
| Residuals | No residuals or unrestricted remembered-information exception. | Propose deletion or narrowing; any surviving residuals right remains RED. The counterproposal is not acceptance authority. | Any residuals right: immediate RED Legal + IP hold, including narrowed wording. Do not wait for insistence; no automatic acceptance. |
| Representatives | Need-to-know access, equivalent confidentiality obligations, and recipient responsibility for representatives. | Legal may approve a defined professional-duty carveout for independent advisers, without open-ended onward disclosure. | Unrestricted affiliates or financing sources, or a blanket responsibility disclaimer. Legal + business owner. |
| Compelled disclosure | Only disclosure legally required; advance notice and cooperation to the extent lawful. | Notice as soon as legally permitted; reasonable assistance at the discloser's expense if agreed. | An unlawful notice duty or disclosure of more than required. Legal; route any actual order to litigation counsel. |
| Return and destruction | Return or destroy within 30 days of request or end; confirm completion if requested. | Limited legal retention and inaccessible routine backups, continuing confidentiality, no active use; Legal validates feasibility. | Indefinite convenience retention, re-use, or training after exit. Legal + IT; Privacy if personal data is involved. |
| Scope and authority | Mutual routine evaluation only; verified entities, no extra commercial obligations, documented signature authority. | Correct entity details and schedules before release. Modified paper goes to client Legal. | Exclusivity, noncompete, express unlimited liability, export/sanctions alerts or controlled technology: RED specialist hold. |

Five years is the sample standard for ordinary confidentiality after expiry or termination; three years requires Legal review. Trade secrets receive protection while qualifying under applicable law. Apply the highest severity and record exception approval.

## SaaS seller positions

| Issue | Preferred | Fallback | Escalate and owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Access and license scope | Ordered hosted service, named users and affiliates, subscription term and usage limits; separately define any downloadable software. | Defined contractors may use the service for the customer's purpose, with responsibility and usage controls. | Resale, perpetual access, source-code rights or omitted intended investor users. Legal + Product + Sales. |
| Customer data and AI | Customer keeps its data rights. Provider use is limited to the service and documented purposes; align the Order, DPA and product terms. | Specifically defined de-identified analytics only after Privacy/Security validation and no re-identification. | Shared-model training rights, personal data or unsupported residency/deletion promises: RED hold. Legal + Privacy/Security + Product. |
| Warranties and remedies | Material conformity to agreed documentation; professional services performed with reasonable skill and care; feasible cure and defined remedies. | Re-perform deficient services; termination of affected service and an agreed unused-fee refund after failed cure. | Guaranteed investment outcomes, error-free AI, uninterrupted availability or all customer regulatory compliance. Legal + Product + Services. |
| Liability architecture | One aggregate 12-month-fee cap for the affected service, a mutual consequential-loss waiver, and express treatment of exceptions. | A defined and approved higher cap for specified confidentiality/security claims, assessed against value, insurance and risk. | Unlimited exposure, conflicting caps or an indemnity defeating the cap. Legal + Finance; Security for data incidents. |
| IP and indemnity | Defined third-party IP claim protection for authorized use; prompt notice, defense control and license/modify/replace/exit remedies. | Causally limited exclusions for customer modifications, inputs or combinations; no admissions or new duties through settlement without consent. | Platform IP transfer or protection for misuse, investment loss or all regulatory penalties. Legal + Product. |
| SOW and services | Scope, deliverables, dependencies, milestones, fees, objective acceptance and signed change control; distinguish background tools from custom work. | Reasonable test and rejection window with cure; phased acceptance and priced changes tied to specific deliverables. | Unpriced development, undefined tests, impossible milestones or one services dispute stopping all subscription fees. Services + Finance + Legal. |
| Fees and renewal | Order states term, quantities, billing, overage mechanism, notice basis and renewal uplift; discounts follow approval authority. | Approved uplift limit and notice; priced usage true-ups and documented scope reductions. | Hidden auto-renewal, conflicting Order/master terms, or unapproved discounts/free work. Sales + Finance + Legal. |
| Suspension and exit | Suspension for material harm or overdue undisputed amounts, with notice/cure where feasible; workable export and deletion at exit. | Immediate action limited to imminent security harm, followed by notice/restoration; defined backup exceptions. | Suspension of unrelated services or an unusable data-export path. Security + Support + Legal; Privacy for personal data. |

## Hardware supplier issues buyer version

| Issue | Buyer position | Evidence and action | Escalate and owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Specifications and changes | Freeze the specification revision, drawings, bill of materials and approved manufacturing site. Require advance product/process/site change notice and written approval for material changes. | Ask Engineering to define materiality, test impact, stock treatment and who can approve a change. Retain the change ID, cost and effective revision. | Unannounced substitution or a change affecting safety, certification or interoperability. Engineering/Quality + Procurement + Legal. |
| Acceptance tied to tests | Attach measurable factory/site acceptance tests, data, sample size, conditions and pass thresholds. Delivery, installation, beneficial use and payment do not themselves equal acceptance. | Name the test owner, retest deadline, defect log and written acceptance record. Link milestone payment to the stated result, not the supplier's invoice date. | No objective criteria, deemed acceptance before testing, or acceptance despite unresolved critical defects. Engineering/Quality + Legal. |
| Warranty from acceptance | Sample buyer position: 12 months from written acceptance, with agreed repair/replacement/retest and treatment of repaired parts. Confirm storage and commissioning assumptions. | Record the actual agreed trigger, duration, exclusions, claim method, return logistics and remedy deadlines. A different trigger needs a visible business decision. | Warranty starts before the buyer can test, or the exclusive remedy cannot restore essential functionality. Quality + Procurement + Legal. |
| Quality agreement | Attach or cross-reference the quality agreement, revision and precedence. Allocate audit, traceability, nonconformance reporting, corrective action and recall/cooperation duties. | Align technical standards with the purchase agreement; specify who investigates, funds remediation and approves restart. Capture supplier flow-down obligations. | Safety/regulated-product gaps, inconsistent quality schedules or unowned recall decisions. Quality + Engineering + Legal; regulatory specialist if needed. |
| End of life and last time buy | Require usable end-of-life/change notice, a last-order window and documented support/spares plan. Notice periods must match forecast, tooling and obsolescence costs. | Procurement validates demand and inventory exposure; Engineering validates alternatives. State quantities, lead times, cancellations and storage terms in the last-time order. | Single-source discontinuation without replacement qualification or unbounded take-or-pay. Supply Chain + Engineering + Finance + Legal. |
| Liability and exceptions | Read warranty, indemnities, delay remedies, confidentiality, exclusions and the aggregate cap together. Propose defined exceptions or higher caps for specifically allocated risks. | Model plausible exposure and available cover; do not treat a cap figure as expected loss. Record each claim category, aggregation rule and approved cap. | Express unlimited liability, contradictory caps or safety/data/IP risks left unallocated. Senior Legal + Finance; relevant specialist. |
| Insurance and COI | Specify relevant insurance, limits, territories, periods and endorsements with the risk/insurance owner. Obtain a current COI and the evidence needed to verify contractual requirements. | Track policy dates, covered entity, required endorsement and renewal owner. A COI is evidence to check, not a substitute for the underlying policy. | Missing/expired evidence or wording promising cover not in the policy. Insurance/Risk + Procurement + Legal. Texas example: source S3. |
| Delivery risk and price | State the selected Incoterms 2020 rule, exact place/point and suitable transport mode. Separately state title, acceptance, price-change notice and customs responsibilities. | Procurement and Logistics sign off on freight, insurance and landed-cost assumptions. Preserve accepted PO prices unless a documented amendment says otherwise. | Unclear transfer point, mismatched trade term/mode, or unilateral retroactive price changes. Logistics + Finance + Legal. Source S2. |
| Tooling data and IP | Separate buyer tooling/materials, supplier background IP, deliverables, licenses, documentation and access needed for support or a replacement source. | List assets, custodians, permitted uses, return rights and practical access at insolvency/exit, subject to relevant law and third-party rights. | Supplier owns buyer-funded improvements without usable rights, or exit depends on unavailable data. Engineering + IP Legal + Procurement. |
| Payment and continuity | Tie deposit, delivery and acceptance amounts to named evidence. Separate a disputed invoice from undisputed amounts; specify notice, cure and proportionate suspension/exit rights. | Finance validates budget, currency, taxes, invoice requirements and credit exposure. Retain recovery, service transition, spare-parts and open-order actions. | Advance payment without safeguards, disputed acceptance treated as complete, or critical supply stopped without an agreed path. Finance + business owner + Legal. |

## Clause revisions

Red strikeout removes text; blue underline adds text in the PDF. All clauses are newly written examples.

### NDA purpose and model use

**Before:** Recipient may use Confidential Information for any internal business purpose, including training shared models.

**Redline:** Recipient may use Confidential Information ~~for any internal business purpose, including training shared models~~ **solely to evaluate the Project and may not use it to train a shared model**.

**Reason and owner:** This revision keeps use within the evaluation purpose. Shared-model training requires RED specialist review under NDA-DEMO-1.0. Owner: Legal + Product + Privacy/Security.

### Hardware acceptance and warranty

**Before:** Goods are accepted on delivery. The warranty ends twelve months after shipment.

**Redline:** Goods are accepted ~~on delivery~~ **only after the Buyer's written confirmation that the Schedule 2 tests passed**. The warranty ends twelve months after ~~shipment~~ **written acceptance**.

**Reason and owner:** This ties payment and remedy timing to evidence of usable performance. Engineering must approve the test schedule and warranty assumptions. Owner: Quality + Procurement + Legal.

### Cloud liability structure

**Before:** All claims, including confidentiality claims, are subject to an aggregate cap of twelve months of fees.

**Redline:** **Except for the separately defined Security Claims cap in Schedule 3,** all claims ~~, including confidentiality claims,~~ are subject to an aggregate cap of twelve months of fees.

**Reason and owner:** This revision separates the general cap from a defined security-claims cap. Schedule 3 must specify scope, amount and aggregation. Owner: Legal + Finance + Security.

## Negotiation judgment cases

### Cloud cap and confidentiality

**Cloud agreement review:** I identified a mismatch between a provider's liability cap and a separate confidentiality remedy and recommended an increase. The signed agreement raised the cap. Its terms included a six-hour repair commitment and service credits.

**Illustration:** Illustrative comparison: a USD240,000 general cap sits beside a USD1,000,000 stated confidentiality remedy. The USD760,000 difference makes the precedence question visible; recoverability depends on the agreed wording and applicable law.

Decision: resolve precedence and define the cap or carveout for the relevant claim; align indemnities and exclusions, then record Legal/Finance approval.

### Subscription terms from both sides

**Customer-side subscription review:** I reviewed an enterprise subscription order and general terms from a global software vendor, identifying overage billing, renewal uplift, suspension, limited remedies and a fee-based liability cap.

**Illustration:** Seller-side preparation: show usage measurement and notice, the effect of reduced scope on renewal price, the cure/termination route for repeated service failure and treatment of data claims.

Decision: make usage, pricing and remedy mechanics explicit, and align the Order with the master terms. Sales/Deal Desk approves pricing; Legal makes the words match.

### Battery management technology license

**Patent and technology license:** I negotiated a cross-border patent and technology license, separating ownership of improvements from use rights and addressing sublicensing and staged payments. Both parties signed.

**Illustration:** Sample structure: Northbank retains background technology; Aster Vale owns its new improvements, with separately defined use rights. Affiliates and approved manufacturing partners receive bounded sublicenses. A fictional USD120,000 fee is split between signature and verified technology transfer.

Decision: settle ownership, use, sublicensing and payment as four linked questions. Confirm patent schedules, transfer evidence and any export-control review before execution.

## MSA and SOW checklist

- Identify the right entity, business owner, paper version and transaction purpose.
- Map the MSA, Order, SOW, DPA, SLA and schedules; resolve precedence.
- State deliverables, exclusions, dependencies and change control.
- Set objective acceptance, rejection, cure and retest steps.
- Check IP ownership, background tools and usable licenses.
- Validate data scope, AI use, security commitments and exit feasibility.
- Reconcile fees, taxes, milestones, invoice evidence and renewal notices.
- Read warranties, indemnities, remedies, exclusions and caps together.
- Record fallback authority, specialist approvals and remaining exceptions.
- Retain final clean text, exhibits, approval record and signed version.

## Construction subcontract checklist

- Define site, scope, drawings, specification revision and programme.
- Align prime-contract flow-downs; identify terms the subcontractor cannot perform.
- Separate variations, change orders, rates and authorized instructions.
- Tie progress/acceptance payment to stated evidence and sign-off.
- Review delay, extension, notice, cure and termination mechanics.
- Allocate safety, access, supervision and quality responsibilities.
- Check licensing, permits and employment-related obligations with local specialists.
- Match insurance requirements to policy/endorsement evidence, not COI labels.
- Check indemnities and caps against project value and insurable exposure.
- Set handover, defects, warranty, close-out and record-retention duties.
