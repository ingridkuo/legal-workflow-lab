# KA-02 Self-service NDA checklist

Use this article when a business user wants to sign an NDA without Legal review.

1. The NDA is on the current approved template, with no changes.
2. The purpose is routine and appears on the list of approved purposes.
3. The counterparty entity and the signer's authority are verified.
4. Any change to the template, including a residuals clause, goes to Legal review. (CT-2, Section 1)
