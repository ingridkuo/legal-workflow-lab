# KA-01 Records retention or export request from an outside sender

Use this article when someone outside the company asks us to keep, produce or export account records.

1. Do not confirm whether the account exists, and do not send any data.
2. Ask the system owner to pause routine deletion for the named account until Counsel decides whether a preservation hold applies. Pausing deletion decides no legal duty. (RD-1, Section 2)
3. Verify the sender's identity and authority through an approved channel: a contact already on file for the account, or a channel Counsel names. The reply address of the request is not an approved channel. (RD-1, Section 3)
4. Open an escalation record that keeps known facts, unknowns, the owner, the next action and the current decision separate, and name the reviewer. (RD-1, Section 4)
5. Counsel decides the preservation and disclosure scope. Intake does not.
