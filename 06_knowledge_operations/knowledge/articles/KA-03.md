# KA-03 Calculating a renewal or termination notice date

Use this article before a notice date goes into the contract calendar.

1. Work from the executed agreement and every amendment or order form, not from a summary. (CT-2, Section 2)
2. Record the notice period, the method of notice and whether the days are calendar days or business days.
3. Count back from the renewal or effective date on the stated basis. If the basis is unclear, Counsel calculates the date.
4. Enter the date in the calendar with the source clause and the name of the person who checked it.
