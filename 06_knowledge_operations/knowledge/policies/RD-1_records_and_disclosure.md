# RD-1 Records and disclosure policy

Fictional policy for this sample. Version 3.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to requests from outside the company to keep, produce or export account records.

## 2. Preservation requests

When a request asks us to keep records, Legal Intake asks the system owner to pause routine deletion for the named account until Counsel decides whether a preservation hold applies. Pausing deletion does not confirm that the account exists and does not decide any legal duty.

## 3. Disclosure and export

No account data is disclosed or exported to an outside requester until Legal Intake has verified the requester's identity and authority through an approved channel and Counsel has approved the scope. An approved channel is a contact already on file for the account or a channel Counsel names. The reply address of a request is not an approved channel.

## 4. Escalation records

Each escalation record keeps known facts, unknowns, the named owner, the next action and the current decision separate. The named reviewer is a different person from the intake owner.
