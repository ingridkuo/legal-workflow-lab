# CT-2 Contract handling policy

Fictional policy for this sample. Version 5.

## 1. Self-service NDAs

A business user may sign an NDA without Legal review only when it is on the current approved template with no changes, for a listed routine purpose, with a verified counterparty and signer. Any change to the template, including a residuals clause, requires Legal review.

## 2. Notice dates

Notice dates are calculated from the executed agreement and every amendment or order form, using the stated method of notice and counting basis. If the basis is unclear, Counsel calculates the date.
