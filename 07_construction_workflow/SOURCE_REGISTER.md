# Construction contract source register

Reviewed November 11, 2026. The issues list compares these model editions; project amendments and governing law require separate analysis.

| Source | Edition | Provisions used |
|---|---|---|
| [AIA official A201-2017 preview](https://lp.aiacontracts.com/hubfs/Preview_A201-2017.pdf?hsLang=en) | A201-2017 | Articles 7-9, 14 and 15, including §§7.3, 9.3.2, 14.2.2, 14.4.3 and 15.1.7 |
| [Taiwan PCC official model/version page](https://www.pcc.gov.tw/content/index?eid=9812&type=C) | Engineering procurement model, 1141230 (December 30, 2025) | Articles 5, 7, 17, 20 and 21; official DOCX read from the listed attachment |

The AIA provisions were checked against its official preview. The Taiwan comparison was checked against the official PCC DOCX, rather than relying only on an institutional adaptation. Source editions are identified; the AIA preview is not redistributed here.

The analysis preserves these qualifications: the three-month negotiation period is a model default; retention and delay damages depend on the relevant selections; and a convenience-termination fee under A201 §14.4.3 depends on the Agreement.
